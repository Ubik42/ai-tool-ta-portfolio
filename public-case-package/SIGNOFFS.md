## Guardrails

- AI suggestions remain advisory for rule copy, visual summaries, and review narratives.
- Deterministic checks own schema validity, DCC rule state, texture mutation, dependency impact paths, and release gates.
- Per-target publish actions stay separate: visual evidence can be safe while texture and engine release remain receipt-gated.
- R8.7 production handoff reuses the same signed boundary; owner approvals stay requested, blocked variants remain held, and retry is only allowed through the ledger.
- R8.8 signed receipts stay sandbox-only, adapter smoke records zero write attempts, and rollback receipts cannot advance waiting-owner payloads.
- R8.9 credential probes keep production secrets outside the public package, review records stay retained, and cross-module release lanes remain dry-run until owner probes close.
- R8.10 adapter failures are synthetic, receipt lineage has no orphaned nodes, and reviewer packet diff cannot close owner probes by itself.
- R8.11 live adapter readiness remains simulated, owner approvals stay requested until recorded externally, and mutation replay emits dry-run receipts with zero live writes.
- R8.12 production cutover remains a public rehearsal: service identity, endpoint route, and owner unfreeze receipts stay private-owner controlled, monitor streams remain watch-only where required, and emergency stop emits no live writes.
- R8.13 private owner receipt bridge exposes only alias, redaction policy, owner state, and public references; cutover signoff diff copies no private receipt bodies; route shadow replay keeps liveWrites at zero.

The current package uses synthetic public fixtures. It proves workflow logic and reviewer evidence shape, not compatibility with proprietary production assets.
